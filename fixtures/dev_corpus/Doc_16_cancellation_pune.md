# Cancellation and refund policy — Pune

## §1 Scope

This policy governs cancellation and refund of venue reservations made in Pune.

## §2 Full refund window

A booking cancelled more than 96 hours before the scheduled start date receives a full refund of all fees paid, excluding non-refundable third-party deposits.

## §3 Partial refund window

A booking cancelled between 48 and 96 hours before the start date receives a 75 percent refund. Cancellations inside 48 hours are non-refundable.

## §4 Rescheduling

A reservation may be rescheduled once at no charge if the request is made more than 96 hours in advance. Subsequent reschedules are treated as a cancellation and rebooking.
