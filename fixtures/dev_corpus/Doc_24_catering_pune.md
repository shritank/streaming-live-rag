# Catering services — Pune

## §1 Approved vendors

Spice Route Foods is the approved on-site catering provider for events in Pune. External caterers require prior written approval from the facilities manager.

## §2 Ordering and lead time

Catering orders must be placed at least 3 business days before the event date, with a minimum of 20 covers. Headcount changes are accepted up to 48 hours before service.

## §3 Dietary accommodation

Spice Route Foods provides vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options on request. Allergen information is supplied per dish; severe allergy accommodation must be arranged directly with the vendor.
