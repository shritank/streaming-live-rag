# Venue A — Hyderabad facility profile

## §1 Overview

Venue A is a conference facility located in Hyderabad. It is used for customer workshops, training sessions and internal offsites.

## §2 Capacity and layout

Venue A supports a maximum workshop capacity of 120 attendees in theatre layout, across 3 breakout rooms. Classroom layout reduces capacity by approximately 30 percent.

## §3 Booking and notice

Reservations for Venue A must be confirmed at least 96 hours in advance. Provisional holds expire automatically after 7 days without written confirmation.

## §4 Facilities

The venue provides projection, wired and wireless microphones, and on-site technical support during business hours. Accessible entrances and step-free access are available at all levels.
