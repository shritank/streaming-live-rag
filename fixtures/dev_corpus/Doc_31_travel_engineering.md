# Travel and expense reimbursement — Engineering

## §1 Standard reimbursement

Employees in Engineering are reimbursed for economy class airfare, standard lodging, and meals up to a per diem of 90 USD per travel day, subject to itemised receipts.

## §2 Submission deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 21 calendar days of the trip end date. Claims filed after this window require line manager justification.

## §3 International travel

International travel requires senior director approval obtained before booking, and introduces a mandatory foreign currency receipt verification step at claim review.

## §4 Non-reimbursable items

Personal entertainment, seat upgrades, traffic fines and companion travel costs are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## §5 Late booking exception

Where a booking is made after travel has commenced, reimbursement requires senior director approval and a written explanation of the exception.
