# Venue B — Jaipur facility profile

## §1 Overview

Venue B is a conference facility located in Jaipur. It is used for customer workshops, training sessions and internal offsites.

## §2 Capacity and layout

Venue B supports a maximum workshop capacity of 30 attendees in theatre layout, across 5 breakout rooms. Classroom layout reduces capacity by approximately 30 percent.

## §3 Booking and notice

Reservations for Venue B must be confirmed at least 72 hours in advance. Provisional holds expire automatically after 7 days without written confirmation.

## §4 Facilities

The venue provides projection, wired and wireless microphones, and on-site technical support during business hours. Accessible entrances and step-free access are available at all levels.
