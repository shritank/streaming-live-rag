# Support service levels — Priority

## §1 Response targets

Priority support requests receive a first response within 24 business hours of ticket creation.

## §2 Resolution targets

Priority incidents are targeted for resolution within 5 business days. Progress updates are provided at least once per business day until closure.

## §3 Escalation path

If a Priority ticket exceeds its resolution target, it is escalated to the duty manager and, after a further business day, to the regional support lead.
